# Microsoft Office AI Features

## Practice Activity

Explore AI-powered document analysis using Microsoft 365 Copilot with three ready-to-use sample documents.

### What is Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Microsoft 365 Copilot is an AI assistant that works with your Office documents and OneDrive files. It can read, analyze, and answer questions about your files, generate summaries, extract insights, and help you work more efficiently with your documents.

### Task

Use Microsoft 365 Copilot to analyze the provided sample documents through natural language queries.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Access Microsoft 365 Copilot
1. Go to https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat
2. Sign in with your Microsoft account
3. Look for the attachment/upload icon in the chat interface

### 2. Choose a Document to Analyze
This folder includes **three ready-to-use sample documents**. Pick one to start with:

- **[AI-Strategy-Meeting-Notes-2026-01-10.docx](AI-Strategy-Meeting-Notes-2026-01-10.docx)** - Meeting notes
- **[AI-Adoption-Impact-Report-Q4-2025.docx](AI-Adoption-Impact-Report-Q4-2025.docx)** - Business report
- **[AI-Tool-Usage-Analytics-Q4-2025.xlsx](AI-Tool-Usage-Analytics-Q4-2025.xlsx)** - Analytics spreadsheet

### 3. Upload and Start Asking Questions
1. Click the attachment icon in Copilot
2. Upload your chosen document
3. Wait for Copilot to process it

## Document 1: AI Strategy Meeting Notes

**File:** [AI-Strategy-Meeting-Notes-2026-01-10.docx](AI-Strategy-Meeting-Notes-2026-01-10.docx)

**What's in it:** Meeting notes from an AI Strategy Committee planning session with 7 action items, 5 key decisions, multiple stakeholders, and 8+ dates.

### Questions to Ask Copilot:

**Extract Action Items:**
- "What are all the action items mentioned in this meeting?"
- "List all action items with their assigned owners and deadlines"
- "Which action items are due in January?"

**Identify Decisions:**
- "What decisions were made in this meeting?"
- "Summarize the key decisions in bullet points"
- "What was decided about the AI training budget?"

**Find Stakeholders:**
- "Who are the key people mentioned in this meeting?"
- "List all stakeholders both internal and external"
- "What role does Sarah Chen have in this initiative?"

**Extract Dates & Deadlines:**
- "What are all the deadlines mentioned?"
- "Create a timeline of all important dates"
- "What needs to be completed by the end of January?"

**Summarize Content:**
- "Summarize this meeting in 3 bullet points"
- "What were the main topics discussed?"
- "What are the risks and concerns raised?"

**Deep Dive:**
- "What is the proposed budget for AI training?"
- "What compliance issues were discussed?"
- "What new AI initiatives are planned for 2026?"

## Advanced Techniques

### Ask Follow-up Questions
Once you get an initial response, dig deeper:
- "Can you elaborate on that point?"
- "What else does the document say about AI governance?"
- "Show me more details about that section"

### Request Different Formats
- "Create a table summarizing the main points"
- "Give me this as a numbered list"
- "Format this as an email I can send to my team"
- "Create a bullet point summary"

### Cross-Document Analysis
Upload multiple documents and compare:
- "Compare the decisions in the meeting notes with the recommendations in the report"
- "Do the meeting notes align with the data in the spreadsheet?"
- "What insights from the Excel data support the report's findings?"

---

## Tips for Better Results

- **Be specific** - Instead of "summarize," try "summarize in 5 bullet points focusing on financial data"
- **Use natural language** - Write as if you're asking a colleague
- **Ask one thing at a time** - Break complex questions into smaller queries
- **Iterate** - If the answer isn't quite right, rephrase your question
- **Reference specific parts** - "What does Section 4 say about..." or "In the Cost Analysis sheet..."
- **Try different angles** - If one question doesn't work, approach it differently
