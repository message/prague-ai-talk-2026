# NotebookLM Practice

## Practice Activity

Explore Google's NotebookLM with Transcarent links for AI-powered note-taking, research, and content synthesis.

### What is NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is Google's AI-powered research and note-taking tool that helps you understand, synthesize, and create content from your sources. It can generate summaries, answer questions, create audio overviews, and more.

### Task

Use NotebookLM to analyze the Transcarent company links and create various AI-generated outputs.

### Steps to Try

#### 1. Set Up Your Notebook
1. Go to https://notebooklm.google.com/
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "New notebook" to create a fresh workspace
4. Give your notebook a descriptive name (e.g., "Transcarent Research")

#### 2. Add Sources
1. Open the [transcarent-links.txt](transcarent-links.txt) file in this folder
2. In NotebookLM, click "Add source"
3. Choose "Web URL" and paste the Transcarent links one by one
4. Wait for NotebookLM to process each source
5. You should see all sources listed in the left panel

#### 3. Explore AI Features

**Create a Summary:**
- Click "Summarize" or ask: "Provide a comprehensive summary of all sources"
- Try: "What are the key points about Transcarent's business model?"

**Generate Audio Overview:**
- Click "Audio overview" button (or "Generate" in the audio section)
- NotebookLM will create a podcast-style conversation discussing your sources
- Listen to how AI transforms written content into natural dialogue
- This typically takes a few minutes to generate

**Create Study Guides:**
- Ask: "Create a study guide based on these sources"
- Try: "What are the most important facts to remember about Transcarent?"

**Ask Specific Questions:**
- "What services does Transcarent provide?"
- "Who are Transcarent's target customers?"
- "What makes Transcarent different from competitors?"
- "Summarize Transcarent's mission and vision"

**Generate a Mind Map:**
- Ask: "Create a mind map of Transcarent's key business areas"
- Try: "Visualize the relationships between different aspects of the company"

#### 4. Advanced Features to Try

**Compare and Contrast:**
- "Compare information from different sources about [specific topic]"

**Create Different Formats:**
- "Write a brief executive summary"
- "Create a FAQ based on these sources"
- "Generate key takeaways in bullet points"

**Citation Checking:**
- Click on any statement in NotebookLM's responses to see which source it came from
- This helps verify information and understand context

### Tips for Better Results

- **Use the audio overview** - It's one of NotebookLM's most powerful features for understanding complex topics
- **Check citations** - Always verify which source information came from
- **Iterate on prompts** - If you don't get what you need, rephrase your question
- **Combine sources** - Ask questions that require synthesizing information across multiple links
